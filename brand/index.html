The direction

Quiet luxury. Loud secrets.

Matte black, real gold foil, a classic serif and a lot of empty space. It should feel like the invitation arrived in a heavy envelope. The trick is the tone: the look is black-card, the voice is your friend at the bar. That keeps it exclusive without getting bougie.

I

Say less

One image, one line, one button per screen. Luxury brands whisper. So do we.

II

Gold is earned

Gold only touches the wordmark, key lines and the thing you should tap. Everything else stays black and ivory.

III

Velvet rope, no bouncer

It looks members-only. Then it lets everyone in. That surprise is the brand.

Logo system

The face, the word, the mark.

Three pieces that work together. The shadowed face from your reference is the hero art for big moments. The serif wordmark does most of the work. A simple gold line version of the finger and lips covers tiny sizes like the app icon and favicon, where a photo turns to mush.

Voice

Next moves

Before we print anything.

  • Hero art at full size. The face in your reference is low resolution. You'll need it redrawn or regenerated at 3000px+ with rights you own, so it works on posters and in the app stores.
  • Line mark. Mine is a placeholder. Have a designer draw the final one in the same line weight as the fingertip in the hero art.
  • Foil printer. Get quotes for gold-foil stickers and cards early. Foil costs more and takes longer than regular print.
  • Domain and handles. Short matters on a 3-inch sticker: shhh.app, getshhh.com, shhhphx.com.
  • Trademark check on "Shhh" in the app category before ordering anything.
  • Decided. Black and gold (Direction II) is the look. Green and brass is retired.
